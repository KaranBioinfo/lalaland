Every beginning is a small act of faith. You commit words to a page without yet
knowing where they lead, trusting that the path will reveal itself in the
walking. The hardest sentence is almost always the first one.

## Why we hesitate

We hesitate because a blank page holds every possible essay at once, and
choosing one means letting the others go. That loss is quiet but real. The cure
is not confidence; it is permission — permission to write badly, briefly, and
then again tomorrow.

## A practice, not a performance

Treat the beginning as a practice rather than a performance. Show up, make a
mark, and let the work accumulate. What looks like inspiration from the outside
is usually just the compounding interest of many unremarkable mornings.
