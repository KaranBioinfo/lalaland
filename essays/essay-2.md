# The Shape of Attention

Attention has a shape. It narrows to a point when we are absorbed and widens to
a horizon when we are at rest. Most of modern life is an argument about which of
those shapes you will hold, and for how long, and on whose terms.

## The economics of a glance

A glance is cheap to give and expensive to recover. Whole industries are built
on the difference. To guard your attention is not to withdraw from the world but
to decide, deliberately, where its light will fall.

## Depth as a decision

Depth is less a talent than a decision renewed each hour. We go deep by
declining the next easy distraction, and then the one after that, until the
quiet work in front of us becomes the most interesting thing in the room.
